## 1) Sub-sectors and primary conversion

### Takeaway
Three groups with different main conversions: **professionals** = consultation request / call (trust built by people, credentials and areas of practice); **real estate agencies** = property enquiry on a listing, plus valuation requests ("valutazione gratuita") from sellers; **local shops** = a visit to the shop (address, hours, map link, phone/WhatsApp), and for small e-commerce = buying a product. Reviews matter in every group. Hard Italian data per sector is thin: word of mouth still dominates for professionals.

### Cited Findings
- 97% of consumers read online reviews of local businesses; 41% "always" read them in 2026, up from 29% the year before (BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey 2026, US sample) — [BrightLocal 2026](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/2026/)
- A third-party summary of the same survey: sample of 1,002 US adults (Feb 2026); 74% only care about reviews from the last three months (not confirmed on BrightLocal's own page) — [PinMeTo summary](https://www.pinmeto.com/news/brightlocal-local-consumer-review-survey-2026/)
- 54% of consumers visit a business's website after reading a good review (BrightLocal, as reported; survey year not clear in the snippet) — [DesignRush](https://news.designrush.com/small-business-website-after-good-review)
- Italian small firms of commercialisti: word of mouth remains central for new client relationships, even when studios invest in updated websites and digital marketing — [Il Sole 24 Ore](https://en.ilsole24ore.com/art/anche-piccoli-si-specializzano-dialogo-rete-i-clienti-AEg7oVLD); [EcNews "La potenza del passaparola"](https://www.ecnews.it/legale/mondo-professione/ai-digitalizzazione/la-potenza-del-passaparola/)
- A trade article says "90% of people trust online reviews" for choosing a commercialista, but gives no source (low reliability) — [TeamSystem magazine](https://www.teamsystem.com/magazine/gestione-dello-studio/commercialisti-passaparola-digitale/)
- Consumer guide for choosing an online commercialista: check how many reviews there are and how varied; many very short 5-star reviews are suspicious — [Fiscozen](https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/commercialista-online-recensioni/)
- Getrix (the management software of Immobiliare.it) is described as the most widely used software among Italian agents; listings edited in Getrix update Immobiliare.it automatically (and also show on TrovaCasa) — [Immobiliare.it software gestionali](https://www.immobiliare.it/info/software-gestionali-consigliati/); [Immobiliare.it software immobiliare](https://www.immobiliare.it/en/info/software-immobiliare/)

### Inferences
- Primary conversion and the CTA to detect, per group:

| Sub-group | Primary conversion | Secondary | Typical Italian CTA strings |
|---|---|---|---|
| Avvocato, commercialista, consulente del lavoro, notaio | Consultation request (form/phone/email) | Call, WhatsApp, PEC for formal contacts | "Richiedi una consulenza", "Prenota un appuntamento", "Contattaci", "Fissa un incontro", "Chiama lo studio" |
| Notaio | Call / email to ask for a quote on a deed ("preventivo atto", "rogito") | Document checklist download | "Richiedi preventivo", "Documenti necessari" |
| Architetto, geometra | Project / site-visit request, portfolio viewing | Call | "Richiedi un sopralluogo", "Progetti", "Portfolio", "Pratiche edilizie" |
| Agenzia immobiliare | Enquiry on a property listing | Seller lead: free valuation | "Richiedi informazioni", "Prenota una visita", "Valutazione gratuita", "Vendi casa" |
| Negozio (abbigliamento, ottico, fioraio, ferramenta, libreria) | Visit to the shop | Call / WhatsApp, booking (ottico: eye test), reservation of an item | "Vieni a trovarci", "Orari", "Come raggiungerci", "Prenota visita optometrica", "Ordina su WhatsApp" |
| Small e-commerce of a local shop | Product purchase | Click & collect ("ritiro in negozio"), newsletter | "Aggiungi al carrello", "Acquista", "Ritiro in negozio" |
- For professionals, the site's job is mostly to **confirm** a referral ("is this person real, qualified, close by, reachable?"). So people, credentials, Albo number, address and a clear contact path count more than marketing copy. This is an inference from the word-of-mouth findings, not a measured result.
- For agencies, the listings are usually on the portals too, so the agency site competes with Immobiliare.it/Idealista. The site's distinctive value is the **seller lead** (valuation) and trust in the team and area.

## 2) Must-have elements ranked by impact (with evidence, detectability, signals)

### Takeaway
Rank = (legal risk if missing) + (conversion impact). Top tier everywhere: identity and legal data in the footer (P.IVA, for companies REA/sede/capitale), a working primary contact (tel: link, form, WhatsApp), address with map link and hours (shops, agencies), and reviews. Then group-specific items: Albo/Ordine and areas of practice for professionals; APE/energy class in every listing for agencies; Codice del Consumo pages, prices with VAT and the new withdrawal button for e-commerce.

### Cited Findings
- P.IVA must be shown on the **homepage** of the website (art. 35 c.1 DPR 633/72); it applies to all VAT-registered subjects, also when the site is only promotional (Risoluzione AdE 60/E of 16 May 2006); exemption only for Italian companies of multinational groups without a site specific to the Italian activity — [fiscoetasse](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/normativa-prassi/1004-partita-iva-sito-web.html); [diritto.it](https://www.diritto.it/l-indicazione-della-partita-iva-sul-sito-web-e-obbligatoria/); [filodiritto](https://www.filodiritto.com/indicazione-numero-partita-iva-nel-sito-web-articolo-35-comma-1-del-dprn-633-del-1972)
- Sanction for missing P.IVA: sources disagree — €250–2,000 (art. 11 D.Lgs. 471/1997, as reported by one source) vs €258.23–2,065.83 (older figure) — [fiscomania](https://fiscomania.com/indicazioni-obbligatorie-siti-web); [punto-informatico](https://www.punto-informatico.it/niente-piva-in-home-page-multato/) **[VERIFY]**
- Reviews: 97% read reviews; recency matters (74% only last 3 months, third-party figure) — [BrightLocal 2026](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/2026/); [PinMeTo](https://www.pinmeto.com/news/brightlocal-local-consumer-review-survey-2026/)
- Avvocati may **not** name their clients on the site or in newsletters, even with the client's consent (art. 35 c.8 CDF, confirmed by CNF decisions) — [Il Sole 24 Ore NT+](https://ntplusdiritto.ilsole24ore.com/art/giro-vite-cnf-informazioni-rese-studi-legali-AGtQFc6B); [diritto.it](https://www.diritto.it/?p=33285)
- Property ads on any commercial medium must show energy performance index and energy class (art. 6 D.Lgs. 192/2005; obligation since 1 Jan 2012, later rewritten in 2013) — [ANCE Emilia-Romagna](https://emiliaromagna.ance.it/2012/01/23/obbligo-di-indicazione-della-classe-energetica-negli-annunci-di-vendita/?print=pdf); [ANCE 2014](https://ance.it/2014/01/modificata-la-normativa-in-materia-di-attestato-di-prestazione-energetica/?print=pdf)
- Every price-reduction announcement must show the lowest price applied in the previous 30 days (art. 17-bis Codice del Consumo) — [MIMIT FAQ](https://www.mimit.gov.it/it/assistenza/domande-frequenti/annunci-di-riduzione-di-prezzo-domande-frequenti-faq)
- From 19 June 2026 online sellers must offer a digital withdrawal function (with confirmation and receipt), new art. 54-bis Codice del Consumo introduced by D.Lgs. 209/2025; pre-contract information (art. 49) must say where it is and how it works — [PMI.it](https://www.pmi.it/impresa/normativa/496173/pulsante-recesso-online-obbligatorio-ecommerce.html) **[VERIFY wording on Normattiva]**

## 3) Legal rules with sources

### Takeaway
Professionals: informational advertising is allowed by any means if true, correct and not misleading; avvocati cannot name clients; commercialisti' code (in force 1 April 2024) allows advertising of activity, specialisations, titles, studio structure and fees but bans commercial/advertising references on the professional's own website (interpretation to verify). Agencies: energy class and IPE in every property ad; REA number shown as good practice. E-commerce: Codice del Consumo info duties, 14-day withdrawal, 30-day lowest price on discounts, new online withdrawal button from 19 June 2026; ODR link no longer required since 20 July 2025; EAA accessibility applies to e-commerce except microenterprises (<10 staff AND turnover or balance ≤ €2M). P.IVA on homepage for everyone (renumbering coming in 2027).

### Cited Findings

**All sectors**
- P.IVA in homepage: art. 35 c.1 DPR 633/72; R.M. 60/E/2006 — [fiscoetasse](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/normativa-prassi/1004-partita-iva-sito-web.html); [diritto.it](https://www.diritto.it/l-indicazione-della-partita-iva-sul-sito-web-e-obbligatoria/)
- New Testo unico IVA (D.Lgs. 19 Jan 2026 n. 10) consolidates DPR 633/72; it applies from **1 January 2027** and renumbers provisions; one source maps the old art. 35 content to new **art. 68** — [informazionefiscale](https://www.informazionefiscale.it/testo-unico-iva-dlgs-10-2026-analisi-novita); [forvismazars](https://www.forvismazars.com/it/en/insights/publications/tax-trends/tax-trends-n.-4-2026/nuovo-testo-unico-iva-dal-2027-le-novita); [miolegale art. 68 (title only)](https://miolegale.it/testi-unici/iva/art-68/) **[VERIFY that the homepage duty is kept in art. 68]**
- A corrective decree to the Testo unico IVA was reportedly approved on 28 Oct 2026 (number/status not verified) — [fiscoetasse](https://www.fiscoetasse.com/approfondimenti/17518-iva-e-commerce-2027-forfettari-escluis-dallo-ioss-cambiano-le-regole.html)
- Legge annuale PMI (L. 11 March 2026 n. 34, in force 7 April 2026), Capo IV arts. 18–23: rules against fake/bought/incentivised online reviews; scope is **restaurants and tourist accommodation only**; reviews within 30 days of use, only by real customers; AGCM guidelines (final text reportedly 19 Oct 2026, sources disagree) — [Il Sole 24 Ore NT+](https://ntplusdiritto.ilsole24ore.com/art/legge-pmi-vigore-7-aprile-2026-AINnlONC); [PMI.it](https://www.pmi.it/impresa/business-e-project-management/494403/false-recensioni-online-regole-pmi.html); [Geopop](https://www.geopop.it/recensioni-online-cosa-cambia-con-le-nuove-regole-antitrust-contro-i-giudizi-manipolati/); [ItaliaOggi](https://www.italiaoggi.it/diritto-e-fisco/diritto-e-impresa/recensioni-online-arrivano-le-nuove-regole-cosa-cambia-per-le-imprese-affm0afb). Not directly applicable to our three groups, but general unfair-practice rules (Codice del Consumo) still forbid fake reviews.

**Avvocati — Codice deontologico forense art. 35**

### Inferences
- A **vetrina** site of a professional or shop (no online sale) is not an "e-commerce service" under the EAA, so EAA duties mainly bite on **non-micro** e-commerce. Most Milan local shops (<10 staff, <€2M) are exempt; still build to WCAG 2.1 AA as good practice and because the exemption depends on size data we cannot see from HTML. Scoring rule: don't penalise a missing accessibility statement unless the site sells online and the company looks non-micro.
- For commercialisti, "no commercial or advertising references" on the website is most likely aimed at third-party banners/pop-ups/sponsored content, not at describing services (the same art. 44 allows advertising services and fees). Rubric: flag third-party ads/banners on a commercialista's site; do not flag service descriptions. **Needs verification of the exact c.11 wording.**
- For avvocati, a rubric must **not reward named client logos/case studies**; testimonials that identify clients are a deontological risk (comma 8). Anonymous testimonials and Google reviews are a grey area (not researched).
- "Classe energetica: in fase di richiesta/rilascio" on listings is a common pattern that likely does not satisfy the ad obligation; flag it as risk.
- From 2027 the legal citation for the P.IVA rule may change (art. 68 TU IVA); the rubric should cite "obbligo P.IVA in homepage (art. 35 DPR 633/72; dal 2027 TU IVA)" until verified.

## 5) Schema.org types

### Takeaway
Use `LegalService` for law firms (schema.org marks `Attorney` as deprecated in favour of `LegalService`), `Notary`, `AccountingService` for commercialisti, `RealEstateAgent` for agencies, `Store` subtypes for shops, and `Product` + `Offer` (price, priceCurrency EUR, availability) for e-commerce. `ProfessionalService` is deprecated for local businesses.

### Cited Findings
- `Attorney` is deprecated; schema.org says `LegalService` is "more inclusive and less ambiguous" (as mirrored in schema library docs) — [pub.dev schema_org Attorney](https://pub.dev/documentation/schema_org/1.0.2/schemas_attorney); [metacpan SemanticWeb::Schema::Attorney](https://web.do.metacpan.org/pod/SemanticWeb::Schema::Attorney)
- `ProfessionalService` is deprecated for local businesses because of confusion with `Service`; it used to group Dentist, AccountingService, Attorney, Notary and others — [attic.schema.org ProfessionalService](https://attic.schema.org/ProfessionalService)

### Inferences (from schema.org knowledge, **verify current hierarchy on schema.org**)
- Professionals: `LegalService` (avvocati); `Notary` (subtype of LegalService); `AccountingService` (subtype of FinancialService) for commercialisti and possibly consulenti del lavoro; architetti/geometri have no dedicated type — use `LocalBusiness` (or `ProfessionalService` despite deprecation) plus `Person` with `jobTitle`, `memberOf` (Ordine), `hasCredential`.
- Agencies: `RealEstateAgent` (LocalBusiness); listings as `Offer`/`Product` or `Residence`/`Apartment`/`House` + `Offer` (Google has no rich result for listings).
- Shops: `Store` subtypes `ClothingStore`, `BookStore`, `Florist`, `HardwareStore`; ottico → `Optician` (MedicalBusiness subtype — to verify).
- Common properties for all: `name`, `address` (PostalAddress, `postalCode` 201xx), `telephone`, `openingHoursSpecification`, `geo`, `url`, `image`, `priceRange`, `vatID`, `taxID`, `sameAs` (Google Business Profile, portals), `aggregateRating` only if reviews are shown on the page (self-serving reviews for LocalBusiness are not eligible for Google review stars — recollection, verify).
- E-commerce: `Product` with `offers` → `Offer` (`price`, `priceCurrency: EUR`, `availability`, `hasMerchantReturnPolicy`, `shippingDetails`).

- Could not fetch schema.org pages; Notary and Optician placement not confirmed by a source in this session.

## 6) Tone and common mistakes

### Takeaway
Professionals: sober, factual, "Lei" or impersonal register, no superlatives or guarantees of outcome, no client names (avvocati), no third-party ads (commercialisti). Agencies: clear listing data, energy class always present, honest photos and prices. Shops: warm, local, practical (hours, how to get there). Common mistakes are mostly legal omissions and stale or misleading content.

### Cited Findings
- Lawyers' information must be true, transparent, respectful of secrecy and confidentiality; no client names — [Il Sole 24 Ore NT+](https://ntplusdiritto.ilsole24ore.com/art/giro-vite-cnf-informazioni-rese-studi-legali-AGtQFc6B); [studiocataldi](https://www.studiocataldi.it/articoli/21983-avvocati-in-vigore-le-modifiche-al-codice-deontologico.asp)
- Commercialisti: advertising must be truthful, decorous, never misleading; no commercial/advertising references on the studio website — [fisco7](https://www.fisco7.it/2025/11/codice-deontologico-commercialisti-e-promozione-dello-studio-professionale-cosa-si-puo-e-non-si-puo-fare/); [ItaliaOggi](https://www.italiaoggi.it/diritto-e-fisco/professioni/commercialisti-come-fare-pubblicita-allo-studio-senza-rischi-omhijgmt)
- Comparative advertising by professionals has limits (CNDCEC 2019) — [IPSOA 2019](https://www.ipsoa.it/documents/quotidiano/2019/04/01/professionisti-pubblicita-comparativa-limiti-ammissibilita)
- Discount displays: most shops show a generic "prezzo precedente" instead of the 30-day lowest price — [consumer.bz.it](https://consumer.bz.it/it/print/pdf/node/35518)
- Stale ODR links after 20 July 2025 risk being misleading — [NatLawReview](https://natlawreview.com/article/no-one-wants-dispute-consumers-end-european-online-dispute-resolution-platform-odr)

### Inferences — common mistakes to score (checkable)
1. P.IVA missing from homepage, or only on "Contatti" page (Y).
2. Company footer incomplete (no REA, capital, PEC) (Y).
3. Avvocato site with client logos / named case studies (P: `I nostri clienti`, `Hanno scelto noi`, logo grids).
4. "Avvocato specialista" / "esperto in" without the official specialist title (DM 144/2015 on specialist titles — recollection, unverified) (P: regex `specialista in`).
5. Commercialista site with third-party banners/ads (Y: ad network scripts `googlesyndication`, `doubleclick`).
6. Superlatives/guarantees: `il migliore`, `n\.?\s?1`, `garantiamo il risultato`, `100% vinte` (Y) — risk under "not misleading".
7. Listings without energy class or with "in fase di richiesta" (Y on listing page).
8. Map embed and Google Fonts loading third-party resources on page load (Y).
9. Hours missing, or different from Google Business Profile (P/N).
10. Old ODR link, old "Codice del Consumo" pages without 30-day price rule or withdrawal button (Y/P).
11. Contact only via generic form, no `tel:` link on mobile (Y).
12. "Lorem ipsum", "Coming soon", copyright year far in the past (`© 20(1\d|20)`) (Y) — staleness signal.
13. Stock photos of "handshake/gavel" instead of real people (N, could use reverse image search).
14. Tone mismatch: overly salesy English-style copy for notai/commercialisti; shops too formal (N).

### Gaps
- No Italian study on tone preferences in these sectors was found; tone norms above are inferred from deontological rules.
