# Prezzi degli extra per siti web di piccole attività locali in Italia (2025-2026)

> **Method caveat (read first).** During this session, direct page fetching (WebFetch/curl) was blocked by the sandbox's egress policy (DNS/CONNECT denied). Every figure below comes from **search-engine extracts of the cited pages** (queries run 13 Oct 2026), not from reading the full pages myself. Each figure is tied to its page URL. Where an extract gave a number without making clear which page it came from, I say so. Before publishing any number, open the source page and check it. Unless noted, all figures are **marketing or list prices published by sellers**, not independent survey data.
>
> **VAT shorthand:** "+IVA" means the source says the price excludes VAT (22%). "IVA esente" or "al netto bollo 2 €" usually means a freelancer in the *regime forfettario*, who charges no VAT. No mark means the extract did not say.

## Q1. What does each add-on cost on top of a base small-business site?

### Takeaway
Few Italian sellers publish per-feature prices, and those who do tend to be low-cost or productised (Register.it, Metodo Sito Web, crearesitoweb.it, vdvgrafica, Vizo, sitiperformanti). Their figures land in these bands:
- Extra page: about **35–150 €**
- Extra language: **100–450 €**. Agencies quote 300–1.500 € when translation is included.
- Booking-engine integration: **from ~300 €**, plus the vendor's own fee of **~15–80 €/month**
- Google Business Profile setup: **~100–500 €** one-off
- Local SEO setup: **~500–1.500 €**
- Professional photography: **~350–950 €** for a small property
- Logo: **~150–500 €** from a freelancer
- Copy per page: **~100–300 €**
- iubenda: **~60–240 €/year** net of VAT

## Q2. How do Italian studios phrase "a partire da" and ranges, and do they publish per-feature prices?

### Takeaway
The dominant pattern is tiered packages labelled "da X €" or "a partire da X €", plus a min–max range per site type. Per-feature prices are published mainly by low-cost or productised providers and by online "calcola preventivo" tools. Most agencies say the final quote follows an analysis. VAT is shown as "+ IVA", or "IVA esente" / "al netto del bollo" for forfettari.

### Cited Findings
- **"A partire da" add-on phrasing.** Vizo: "servizio add-on di Integrazione Booking Engine (**a partire da 300 €**)". — [vizo.it](https://www.vizo.it/quanto-costa-un-sito-web-per-hotel/)
- **Min–max table.** Marco Cannia's table shows "€ 900 · € 1.500" for vetrina, with the note "**I prezzi... sono al netto dell'imposta di bollo (2€)**". Price depends on "complessità, funzionalità e obiettivi". — [marcocannia.it](https://www.marcocannia.it/prezzi-siti-web/)
- **"Da" in the title.** "Prezzi realizzazione siti web 2026: **listino da €700**", with tiers "da 699 €", "da 900 €" and footnote "Ogni progetto può essere personalizzato". — [lorenzoalbano.it](https://www.lorenzoalbano.it/web-agency-italiana-prezzi-competitivi/)
- **VAT-exempt listino.** E-designer.it 2026 listino is "indicativo" and depends on "durata del progetto e complessità". "**I prezzi si intendono IVA esenti**". — [e-designer.it](https://e-designer.it/listino-prezzi-grafica/)
- **Online calculators that build the price from components:**
  - Facomunica: base type + €100/page + features + complexity — [facomunica.it](https://facomunica.it/modulo-richiesta-di-preventivo-per-sito-web/)
  - crearesitoweb.it: line items (+€500 design, +€300 multilingual, +€200 SEO, +€400 reserved area, +€30/month maintenance), with a **separate first-year total** — [crearesitoweb.it](https://www.crearesitoweb.it/)
  - CreaCity: tool estimates "based on listini medi" and stresses they vary with the real project — [creacity.it/tool](https://creacity.it/tool/preventivo-sito-web/)
  - SN Web Solution: calls its output "una stima" — [snwebsolution.com](https://snwebsolution.com/preventivo-sito-web.html)
  - Dopstart: issues a quote only after assessing needs — [dopstart.com](https://www.dopstart.com/preventivo-sito-web/)
- **Sub-100 € add-ons.** sitiweba100euro lists add-on services as "**DA 20 €**" one-off. — [sitiweba100euro.it](https://sitiweba100euro.it/preventivo-siti-internet/)
- **Promo crossed-out pricing:**
  - GetHigher 250 € (was 500 €) — [gethigher.it](https://gethigher.it/realizzazione-siti-web-hotel-booking-engine)
  - Online Facile GBP €99 + IVA (was €180) — [onlinefacile.com](https://onlinefacile.com/google-my-business/)
- **Fixed-price model.** Metodo Sito Web advertises costs known in advance, with no final "consuntivo". — [metodositoweb.it](https://www.metodositoweb.it/)
- **Forfettario invoicing rules:**
  - Invoices carry "Operazione in franchigia IVA..." (art. 1, c. 54-89, L. 190/2014), nature N2.2, RF19 — [fiscozen.it](https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/cosa-scrivere-nella-fattura-in-regime-forfettario/)
  - A **2 € bollo** applies above 77,47 €, commonly recharged to the client — [fiscozen.it](https://www.fiscozen.it/guide/pagare-marca-da-bollo-fattura-forfettaria/); [studiopizzano.it](https://www.studiopizzano.it/36-imposta-di-bollo-sulle-fatture-del-regime-forfettario/)

### Inferences
- A calculator labelled "stima del preventivo" that shows "da X €" per item, a clear "IVA esente (regime forfettario)" note (if applicable) plus the 2 € bollo line, and a separate list of recurring third-party costs would match Italian norms. Showing the first-year total separately, as crearesitoweb.it does, is an existing precedent.

### Gaps
- No systematic count of how many Italian freelancers publish per-feature prices; the evidence is anecdotal.
