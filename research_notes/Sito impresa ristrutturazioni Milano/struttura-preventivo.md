# Sito impresa di ristrutturazioni (Milano, 5-20 persone): struttura, flusso preventivo e sopralluogo

> Method note for the report writer: in this session the egress proxy blocked direct page fetches (WebFetch / curl to company sites, baymard.com, etc. all refused). Every finding below comes from web search result summaries of the cited pages, not from my own inspection of the live sites. So the **exact on-page section order** of the example sites could not be checked. What is reported is what the pages claim (offers, guarantees, prices, steps, CTAs). Company claims are self-declared marketing unless stated otherwise.

## 1. Page structure and home section order of strong renovation sites (6-10 real examples)

### Takeaway
Agency roundups (2026) and the sites I could look at follow one pattern. A hero with real finished work, the specialty and the zone served comes first, then trust signals right under it, then services split into one page each, then the portfolio by project type, then the process, reviews, FAQ and a single primary CTA. The best Milan firms compete on "un solo referente, prezzo fisso, data di consegna con penale, sopralluogo gratuito". The best US/UK firms compete on a named step-by-step process, a free consultation of a fixed length, and clear price anchors.

### Cited Findings
**Generic pattern (agency/vendor guides, 2026; opinion, not controlled data)**
- Recommended order: a hero showing completed work + specialty statement + service area, followed immediately by 3-4 trust signals (licence, insurance, Google rating) — [Omnyra, Remodeling Contractor Website Playbook](https://omnyra.ai/blog/remodeling-contractor-website-guide)
- Portfolio should be organised by project type (kitchens, baths, additions). Make a dedicated page per service category, plus location pages such as "Kitchen Remodeling in [City]" with local testimonials — [Omnyra](https://omnyra.ai/blog/remodeling-contractor-website-guide)
- Roundups praise before/after sections as proof of work. They also praise nav bars that break services into separate pages "to create clear CTAs", and service pages that "clearly list the entire process". Named examples: Breyer Construction & Landscape, Eberson Construction, Medford Design Build, Kasper Custom Remodeling, Bill Huey + Associates, ReBath, DreamMaker Bath & Kitchen — [Red Spot Design, Best Home Remodeling Websites 2026](https://www.redspotdesign.com/best-home-remodeling-websites/); [Contractor Gorilla, 16 Best Remodeling Website Designs 2026](https://contractorgorilla.com/blog/best-remodeling-websites/); [Comrade Web](https://comradeweb.com/blog/remodeling-websites/)

### Inferences
- Page list for a 5-20 person Milan firm, derived from the examples above:
  - Home
  - Servizi (one page each: ristrutturazione appartamento, bagno, cucina, plus others actually offered)
  - Lavori/Progetti (index + one case-study page per job)
  - Come lavoriamo (metodo)
  - Prezzi indicativi / Quanto costa (or a section inside each service page)
  - Chi siamo (people + firm data)
  - Zone servite (or Milan quartieri pages, if the work is real)
  - Recensioni
  - FAQ
  - Preventivo / Sopralluogo (the form)
  - Contatti
  - Privacy/Cookie
- Suggested home order, a synthesis of the Omnyra pattern and the Milan firms' trust levers:
  1. Hero: a real finished-work photo, "Ristrutturazioni chiavi in mano a Milano", primary CTA "Prenota il sopralluogo gratuito", secondary WhatsApp/telefono
  2. A trust strip (Google rating + count, years, number of jobs, warranty years, insurance)
  3. Services (cards with a "da X €" price only if the client provides real figures)
  4. Selected works with before/after
  5. Process with times
  6. Guarantees (fixed price, cronoprogramma + penalty, single point of contact)
  7. Reviews
  8. Zones served
  9. FAQ
  10. Final CTA / form
- Milan firms win on promises that can be checked (zone, days, penalty, warranty years). Every number on the site must come from the client (CLAUDE.md rule: no invented facts → TODO).

## 2. Quote flow: one-step vs multi-step, field count, photo upload, WhatsApp, click-to-call, booking a sopralluogo

### Takeaway
For a complex request (type of work, rooms, m², timeline, budget, photos), a short multi-step form tends to beat one long page. Ask the easy, non-personal questions first and the contact details last. But the headline numbers (86%, 300%, 13.9% vs 4.5%) are vendor case studies with weak baselines. In Italy, WhatsApp is the single most preferred channel for talking to companies (about 44-46%), but no channel reaches a majority. So offer form + WhatsApp + phone side by side.

### Cited Findings
- Zuko's view: for long or complex forms, splitting them into steps tends to raise conversion, but the gain varies. Zuko also notes conversion gains of up to 10% from fixing back-button bugs in multi-step flows — [Zuko blog, single page or multi-step](https://zuko.io/blog/single-page-or-multi-step-form)
- Venture Harbour's self-reported multi-step results:
  - a 4-step form with 30+ questions converted 53% of visitors
  - a consulting enquiry form went from 0.96% to 8.1% after moving to multi-step with the same fields
  - an independent review notes the "743%" comparison used a weak baseline (a basic WordPress form), and that the Vendio lift is cited as 59% by Venture Harbour but as 214% by Instapage
  — [Venture Harbour, multi-step lead forms](https://ventureharbour.com/multi-step-lead-forms-get-300-conversions/); [Venture Harbour, 5 studies on form length](https://ventureharbour.com/how-form-length-impacts-conversion-rates/); critique summarised in [milan-novotny.com statistics](https://www.milan-novotny.com/statistics/multi-step-vs-single-step-form-statistics/)
- A Formstack figure is widely repeated: multi-page forms at 13.9% vs single-page at 4.5% (the year of the original study is unclear). A "HubSpot 86% lift" figure is also repeated, origin unverified — [Leadgen Economy](https://www.leadgen-economy.com/blog/multi-step-forms-conversion-optimization/); [Reform](https://reform.app/blog/single-page-vs-multi-step-forms-which-converts-better)
- Conversion Fanatics test: multi-step beat control by 52.9%; a streamlined single-step version gained 40.3% — reported in [Zuko blog](https://zuko.io/blog/single-page-or-multi-step-form)
- HubSpot study of about 40,000 landing pages (older, roughly 2010s):
  - 3 fields converted best (~25%), but the relationship was not linear
  - text areas and drop-downs were linked to drop-off, plain text fields were not
  - asking for a phone number lowered conversion
  — [HubSpot, form fields that kill conversion](https://blog.hubspot.com/blog/tabid/6307/bid/6748/3-form-fields-that-kill-landing-page-conversion-rates.aspx); [HubSpot, field types](https://blog.hubspot.com/blog/tabid/6307/bid/6746/which-types-of-form-fields-lower-landing-page-conversions.aspx)
- A counter-case: Michael Aagaard cut a form from 9 to 6 fields and conversion fell 14%. Another test with 15 fields gave a 109% uplift (lead quality included in the goal). CXL argues that "fewer fields" is not universal — [CXL, Should you really reduce form fields?](https://cxl.com/blog/reduce-form-fields/); [Venture Harbour 5 studies](https://ventureharbour.com/how-form-length-impacts-conversion-rates/)
- Zuko benchmarks:
  - about two-thirds of people who start a form finish it, and about 45% of viewers complete
  - overall conversion about 51.7% across 93M+ sessions
  - highest-abandonment fields: password 10.5%, email 6.4%, phone 6.3%
  — [CueFully summary of Zuko](https://cuefully.com/blog/form-abandonment-statistics); [Zuko industry benchmarking](https://www.zuko.io/benchmarking/industry-benchmarking)
- Italy, WhatsApp, consumer side:
  - Commify Italia (Esendex/Skebby) survey, 1,033 Italian consumers, Jul-Aug 2026: WhatsApp is preferred by 46% for notifications and 44% for pre/post-sale support
  - in the 30-45 age band, SMS edges WhatsApp for support (45% vs 44%)
  - 54-56% choose channels other than WhatsApp, so multichannel matters
  — [Mark-up](https://www.mark-up.it/whatsapp-e-il-canale-preferito-dai-clienti-per-parlare-con-le-aziende/); [CMI Magazine](https://www.cmimagazine.it/31356-whatsapp-e-il-canale-preferito-dai-clienti-italiani-ma-nessuno-basta-da-solo/); [Batista70](https://www.batista70phone.com/2026/09/21/whatsapp-aziende-canale-preferito)
- A separate Esendex study: 83% of Italians are willing to answer a WhatsApp message from a company, more so if the sender is verified and the message personalised. About 70% would drop a brand over poor communications — [CMI Magazine](https://www.cmimagazine.it/31356-whatsapp-e-il-canale-preferito-dai-clienti-italiani-ma-nessuno-basta-da-solo/)
- Italy, WhatsApp, business side (Osservatorio WhatsApp Marketing Italia 2026): 73% of surveyed companies use WhatsApp with clients/prospects; 58.5% only via the free WhatsApp Business App; 14.3% via the official API — reported via [Agenda Digitale](https://www.agendadigitale.eu/?p=282407) / [Corriere Comunicazioni](https://www.corrierecomunicazioni.it/?p=114828) (search summary; I could not open the original report). See also [Megamodo](https://www.megamodo.com/whatsapp-regina-italia-consumatori-avanti-imprese-indietro/) ("consumatori avanti, imprese indietro")
- Practice among real firms:
  - BOWA, Neil Kelly and Normandy all lead with a free in-home consultation of a stated length (60-90 min) as the main conversion step
  - Resi offers a free 30-min phone call and an instant estimator
  - Mediolanum sells a 30-60 min free sopralluogo, followed by a computo metrico within about a week
  — [BOWA](https://www.bowa.com/the-bowa-process-how-to-enjoy-remodeling-phase-1/); [Neil Kelly](https://www.neilkelly.com/design-build-remodeling/exterior/); [Resi](https://resi.co.uk/); [Mediolanum](https://www.mediolanumristrutturazioni.it/)

### Inferences
- Recommended flow: a 3-4 step form.
  1. Type of work (tappable cards: appartamento intero / bagno / cucina / altro)
  2. Zone/CAP + approximate m² + whether the home is lived in
  3. Timing (entro 3 mesi / 3-6 / più avanti) + optional budget band + optional photos
  4. Name + phone or email + preferred contact channel (WhatsApp/telefono/email) + preferred sopralluogo slot (mattina/pomeriggio/sabato)
  - Keep the phone number optional or make it the only required contact field, since HubSpot found phone fields reduce completion. For a builder, though, the phone is often what makes the lead workable, so this is a trade-off to discuss with the client.
- Photo upload should be optional and placed after the commitment step. No evidence was found either way (see Gaps), but upload is high-friction on mobile. A good substitute: "Puoi anche mandarci le foto su WhatsApp" with a prefilled message.
- WhatsApp click-to-chat (a wa.me link with prefilled Italian text, e.g. "Ciao, vorrei un sopralluogo per…"). It needs no third-party script at page load, which fits the CLAUDE.md rule against third-party requests.
- Booking a slot: an embedded third-party calendar (Calendly etc.) would load third-party resources and breaks the project rule. Better: ask for a preferred slot inside the form and confirm by phone or WhatsApp. Or link out to an external booking page.

### Gaps
- No credible study isolated the effect of a **photo upload** field on completion (Zuko has no published upload benchmark that I found).
- No study specific to renovation/home-services lead forms in Italy.
- I found no independent data comparing booked-slot vs "we call you back" for sopralluoghi.
- The Osservatorio WhatsApp Marketing Italia 2026 figures come second-hand from search summaries; the original report was not opened.

## 3. Indicative prices and online estimators: do they help?

### Takeaway
No independent controlled evidence exists. The industry consensus (remodeler marketing press, 2024-2026) is that price ranges or a "starting from" figure pre-qualify leads and raise close rates, and Google is experimenting with surfacing businesses that publish prices. Several Milan competitors already publish bathroom ranges (about 3.500-9.000 €), so having no price at all is now a gap rather than the norm.

### Cited Findings
- Remodelers Advantage argues that price ranges or calculators let buyers screen themselves. For custom work it suggests ranges built from past jobs, real project examples or a simple calculator. It also says hiding prices can leave a site out of AI-search answers — [Remodelers Advantage](https://remodelersadvantage.com/put-pricing-on-your-remodeling-website-for-better-leads/)
- Displayed pricing reportedly means fewer unqualified calls and higher close rates (no data cited). Suggested formats: "starting at", ranges with cost drivers, Good/Better/Best tables — [Kitchen Remodeling SEO](https://kitchenremodelingseo.com/should-i-put-my-prices-on-my-website/)
- ProRemodeler reports Google testing an experimental "online estimates" option in near-me contractor searches that favours transparent pricing — [ProRemodeler](https://www.proremodeler.com/business/article/55383325/how-ai-is-forcing-home-improvement-to-get-honest-about-pricing); also described (vendor) by [Priceguide](https://priceguide.ai/home-service-providers/)
- A vendor case study: a Florida outdoor-living contractor's cost calculator "generated $275k in revenue in 90 days" (self-reported) — [Builder Funnel](https://info.builderfunnel.com/case-studies/how-a-florida-outdoor-space-builder-generated-275k-in-revenue-in-90-days-with-a-cost-calculator)
- A vendor at fieldwork-tool level (Google I/O, 20 May 2026): Google's "Ask for Me" AI agent that calls local businesses to check pricing/availability is reportedly expanding to home repair — [JobNimbus](https://www.jobnimbus.com/blog/google-ai-calling-home-services) (vendor blog, unconfirmed against Google)
- Milan price anchors published by competitors:
  - Ristruttura Milano: bagno da ~3.500 € (≤5 m², no systems) / da 5.500 € (5-7 m², systems redone) — [ristrutturamilano.com blog](https://ristrutturamilano.com/blog/quanto-costa-ristrutturare-bagno-milano)
  - Gama Milano: bagno 4-5 m² 5.000-9.000 € + IVA — [gamamilano.com](https://www.gamamilano.com/costi-medi-ristrutturazioni/)
  - Mirino: "chiavi in mano da € 4.150,00" — [ristrutturazionibagnimirino.it](https://ristrutturazionibagnimirino.it/)
  - Mediolanum: kitchen €1.000/m² + kitchen — [mediolanumristrutturazioni.it](https://www.mediolanumristrutturazioni.it/ristrutturazione/ristrutturazione-cucina/)
  - Portals: bagno a Milano from 3.500 € to over 6.000 € — [Cronoshare](https://www.cronoshare.it/quanto-costa/ristrutturazione-bagno/milano)
- US/UK examples:
  - BOWA publishes a minimum project threshold (~$200k) and a "Guaranteed Budget Range" — [BOWA FAQ](https://www.bowa.com/client-experience/faq-frequently-asked-questions/)
  - Resi publishes tiers (from £1.5k/£3k) and runs an instant estimator plus a build-cost calculator — [Resi pricing](https://resi.co.uk/pricing); [Quick Quote](https://resi.co.uk/quick_quotes/new)
- Older Italian context (around 2015, flag as old): in a Houzz survey of Italian renovators, budget was the top difficulty (47%), ahead of finding materials (45%) and finding the right professionals (27%) — [Houzz Italia](https://www.houzz.it/magazine/houzz-e-home-un-indagine-racconta-come-ristrutturano-gli-italiani-stsetivw-vs~53119591)

### Inferences
- Recommend "prezzi indicativi" as ranges with what is included and what changes the price (m², systems, finishes, building condition). Always add "il prezzo definitivo dopo il sopralluogo, bloccato in contratto". Only use the client's real figures; otherwise write `[TODO: fasce di prezzo reali dal cliente]`.
- A full online estimator is optional. A light "stima orientativa" (type + m² → range) can double as step 1 of the multi-step form. Vendor evidence is positive but self-interested.
- Bottom-only "da X €" pricing risks anchoring the firm as low-cost. Ranges plus real case-study budgets ("budget fascia 40-60k") read as more honest.

### Gaps
- No A/B test or independent study comparing contractor sites with vs without prices.
- No Italian consumer data from 2024-2026 on expectations about online prices.

## 4. Portfolio: how to present works

### Takeaway
The strongest sites treat each job as a case study named after a place (neighbourhood/town + type), grouped by project type. Each one has before/after, scope and, on the best sites, budget/time facts. Before/after is the proof element agencies rate highest. Avoid auto-rotating carousels.

### Cited Findings
- Agencies call before/after sections "a clear demonstration of the work and credibility". They recommend organising the gallery by project type — [Red Spot Design](https://www.redspotdesign.com/best-home-remodeling-websites/); [Omnyra](https://omnyra.ai/blog/remodeling-contractor-website-guide)
- Neil Kelly names project profiles by neighbourhood + scope ("Normandy Park Whole House Remodel, Seattle") — [Neil Kelly project](https://www.neilkelly.com/projects/normandy-park-whole-house-remodel/); [project profile](https://www.neilkelly.com/project-profile-normandy-park-whole-house-seattle/)
- Resi lists 30 case studies named by client + location ("Nick's rear extension", "Richard's Twickenham project") — [Resi case studies](https://resi.co.uk/advice/case-studies)
- BOWA groups projects by category (e.g. "Whole-House Remodels") — [BOWA projects](https://www.bowa.com/projects/whole-house-remodels/)
- Location pages work best with local testimonials — [Omnyra](https://omnyra.ai/blog/remodeling-contractor-website-guide)

## 5. Mobile behaviour: traffic share, sticky call/WhatsApp bar, above-the-fold

### Takeaway
Mobile dominates home-services discovery (commonly quoted at about 70%, but unsourced). In Italy, mobile reaches about 96% of 18-74 year-olds online. So the phone layout is the main layout. Above the fold it should show what the firm does + where + one primary CTA + a one-tap call/WhatsApp option. A sticky bottom bar is common practice, but I found no study on it.

## 6. Process section: steps, timelines, fixed delivery/penalty

### Takeaway
Top US firms make a named, numbered process (5-6 phases, starting with a free consultation of a stated length) the backbone of the site. Milan's leading turnkey firms add contractual guarantees: a fixed itemised price after the sopralluogo, a cronoprogramma attached to the contract with a daily penalty, one point of contact, and multi-year warranties. Nothing measures the conversion effect of a penalty clause, but it is the strongest differentiator among Milan competitors.

### Cited Findings
- Neil Kelly: six phases from first consult to final walkthrough. Phase 1 is a free 60-90 min in-home consultation, then schematic design, then design development (plans, specs, materials finalised), and so on — [Neil Kelly Exterior](https://www.neilkelly.com/design-build-remodeling/exterior/); [Kitchen](https://www.neilkelly.com/design-build-remodeling/kitchen-remodel/)
